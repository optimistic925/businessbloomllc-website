## Material items still requiring policy/counsel confirmation

1. **Refund and cancellation policy.** No universal refund period or digital-product refund rule was invented. Final policy must be approved and then presented consistently before/at checkout and in Terms where appropriate.
2. **Governing law / venue / dispute resolution.** Not invented. Counsel should confirm the appropriate jurisdiction and any arbitration/venue language.
3. **Official legal/contact information.** Confirm the appropriate legal notice/contact channel and any mailing/address disclosures required for the business and applicable jurisdictions.
4. **Privacy rights and request handling.** Confirm applicable jurisdictional rights, identity-verification process, response workflow, and any legally required notices.
5. **Retention schedule.** Current Privacy language is principle-based; operational retention periods/categories should be confirmed against actual systems and legal/business requirements.
6. **Service-provider disclosures.** Confirm the final processor/vendor inventory and whether additional disclosures or data-processing terms are required.
7. **Cookies/analytics.** Confirm the actual production analytics/cookie stack before representing consent or cookie practices.
8. **HR product boundary.** Final HR customer-facing materials should be reviewed to avoid implying legal/employment-law advice and to account for jurisdiction-specific employment requirements.
9. **LLC Foundation boundary.** Final LLC/legal-information materials should clearly remain educational and require qualified legal/tax review where appropriate.
10. **AI product boundary.** Preserve human-review, material-claim verification, sensitive-data, and professional-review language across AI-assisted products.
11. **Marketing/earnings claims.** Public copy must avoid guaranteed earnings, unsupported ROI/performance promises, fabricated testimonials/ratings/customer counts, and false urgency/scarcity.

## Approved source principles already supported by Business Bloom product notices

- AI output may contain errors or fabricated facts and requires human review.
- Material claims should be verified before use.
- Confidential or sensitive data should not be submitted to AI tools unless authorized and appropriately safeguarded.
- Business Bloom educational resources do not replace qualified legal, accounting, tax, investment, medical, regulatory, or other professional advice.
- Customers remain responsible for obtaining qualified guidance where their facts, industry, jurisdiction, transaction, or circumstances require it.
- Customer/prospect/CRM and AI-prompt data should be handled with legitimate-purpose, access-control, consent, retention, deletion, and third-party-platform considerations.
