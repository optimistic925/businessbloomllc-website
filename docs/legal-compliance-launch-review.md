## Concrete pre-launch defects found and corrected

1. **Support contact path** — the Support page previously routed customers to the general Get Started path and did not directly expose the approved support email. Corrected to display and link `support@businessbloomllc.com` with clear purchase/delivery/privacy/Terms support scope.

Those claims and URL-based personal-data transfer were removed.

Corrected to call the trusted Marketplace checkout path and to display concise one-time purchase, Terms, Privacy, final-sale, and fulfillment-error disclosure next to the checkout action.
