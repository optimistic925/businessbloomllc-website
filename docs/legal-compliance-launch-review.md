## Internal completion

The following internal items are complete for the current launch scope:

- Terms of Service — current Marketplace/digital-product scope, one-time purchase model, digital delivery, access responsibilities, license/use restrictions, Free Resources, professional-advice boundaries, AI/human-review requirements, earnings/result disclaimers, refund/cancellation handling, delivery-error handling, chargeback/support process, acceptable use, third-party services, customer responsibility, intellectual property, and future-subscription disclosure language are included.
- Privacy Policy — current website, Marketplace, Free Resource, support, payment, delivery, onboarding, technical/analytics, service-provider, AI/sensitive-information, security, retention/minimization, privacy-request, communications, children, and regional-use concepts are included.
- Refund/cancellation language — completed for the current one-time digital-product model. Default commercial position: final after delivery/access is made available, subject to applicable law and documented fulfillment-error resolution. Duplicate charges, incorrect delivery, corrupted/inaccessible files, and other fulfillment defects are routed to Support for correction or appropriate resolution.
- Subscription language — no subscription is introduced in the current approved Marketplace catalog. Terms require price, renewal interval, cancellation method, and trial/promotional terms to be disclosed before purchase if a recurring offer is introduced later.
- Digital delivery terms — customer email responsibility, link/access handling, saving purchased files, non-public redistribution, and delivery-failure support are included.
- Product licensing consistency — portfolio default license permits purchaser/authorized internal business use and prohibits resale, public redistribution, sublicensing, ownership misrepresentation, and removal of ownership notices unless product-specific terms expressly differ.
- AI boundaries — preserve human review, fact/claim verification, sensitive-data controls, permission requirements, and professional review.
- HR boundaries — Terms expressly state that HR materials are operational/educational and do not replace employment-law review, required notices, or jurisdiction-specific policy review.
- LLC/legal-information boundaries — Terms expressly state that entity-formation, licensing, contract, tax, and related legal-information materials are educational, do not create an attorney-client relationship, and require qualified review when appropriate.
- Marketing/earnings claims — current Terms prohibit treating examples, calculations, scenarios, benchmarks, or projections as guaranteed results and disclaim guaranteed revenue, earnings, sales, funding, conversion, marketing, ranking, productivity, or automation outcomes.
- Customer responsibility — final decisions, permissions/consents, credential protection, backups, claim review, professional review, and production testing remain the customer’s responsibility.
- Customer-facing delivery isolation — Privacy language states that delivery paths are intended to exclude internal production folders, engineering materials, private credentials, unrelated customer data, and Internal-Do-Not-Distribute assets.

2. Confirm whether governing-law, venue, arbitration, class-action waiver, or other dispute-resolution language should be added and, if so, the exact enforceable language and jurisdiction.

5. Confirm privacy-rights, identity-verification, request-response, retention, processor/vendor, international-transfer, analytics/cookie, and consent requirements for the actual production stack and customer geography.
6. Confirm HR product language and customer materials for employment-law and jurisdiction-specific risk.
7. Confirm LLC/entity-formation and legal-information product language for unauthorized-practice, tax, filing, and jurisdiction-specific risk.
8. Confirm portfolio license consistency and any product-specific license exceptions.

10. Confirm whether any recurring/subscription product becomes part of the launch scope; if it does, review renewal, cancellation, trial, billing, and consent disclosures before activation.

## Approved source principles preserved

- AI output may contain errors or fabricated facts and requires human review.
- Material claims should be verified before use.
- Confidential or sensitive data should not be submitted to AI tools unless authorized and appropriately safeguarded.
- Business Bloom educational resources do not replace qualified legal, accounting, tax, investment, medical, HR, employment-law, regulatory, or other professional advice.
- Customers remain responsible for obtaining qualified guidance where their facts, industry, jurisdiction, transaction, workforce, or circumstances require it.
- Customer/prospect/CRM and AI-prompt data should be handled with legitimate-purpose, access-control, consent, retention, deletion, and third-party-platform considerations.
